Privacy policy

The AI

To sort a note, its text and photos are sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which returns a title, a group, a priority and a date. Anthropic processes them for us under its commercial terms and does not use them to train its models. [Check and link Anthropic's current commercial terms and data retention.]

Who helps us (processors)

  • Fly.io: runs our server and stores the data, in Frankfurt, Germany (EU).
  • Anthropic: the AI that sorts notes (see above).
  • Stripe: handles payments for Premium on the web. Your card details go to Stripe, never to us; we keep only your plan and Stripe's customer number.
  • [When subscriptions inside the apps launch:] Apple and Google handle payments made inside the iPhone or Android app.
  • Resend: sends emails such as password reset links (your email address and the message).
  • Sentry: receives error reports from the server (where the error happened and your account number, never the text of your notes or your email).
  • Google, Apple, GitHub and others: only if you choose "Continue with…" to log in.
  • Google, Microsoft, Calendly, Apple (iCloud): only if you connect your calendar there. record.ai reads and writes events in your own account with that service.
  • Apple, Google and Mozilla push services: deliver notifications to your device, encrypted, only if you turn notifications on.
  • Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook: only if you add a note or your calendar link to them yourself.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and notes: until you delete them or your account.
  • Premium backups: 30 days each.
  • When you delete your account: notes, photos, backups, shares, sign-ins, connected calendars and notification settings are deleted at once. Usage statistics are kept without any link to you. Details: Delete your account.

Your rights

You can see, correct, download and delete your data. In the app: ⬇ Download all my notes and Delete my account are under your initials. You can also write to [support@your-domain], object to processing, or complain to the Italian data protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, garanteprivacy.it).

Children

record.ai is for people aged 16 and over. Younger children can use it only with a parent's consent and account. [Check the age for Italy and for the app stores.]

Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only in scrambled form (scrypt). Only you and the people you share with can see your notes.

If this policy changes in an important way, we tell you in the app first.
