Help and support

Write to support@sortmemo.com or, in the app, tap your initials, then 💬 Send feedback. We answer within two working days.

Common questions

How do I make a checklist?

Start each line with - , or paste several lines and choose One note with a checklist.

How do I share a list with my partner?

Open the list and tap 👥 Share, or tap 👥 Share next to a group in the Groups view. Type their email and choose Can edit or View only.

The AI chose the wrong group or date.

Open the note and pick your own group, priority, date or time. Your choice always wins; the AI never changes it back.

How do I connect my calendar?

In the web app at sortmemo.com (the phone apps don't connect calendars yet), tap your initials, then 📅 Calendars, and connect Google, Outlook, Calendly or iCloud. Meetings show up as notes, and notes with a date go to the calendar marked ✍️. For iCloud, make an app-specific password at account.apple.com first. You can also add a one-way calendar link, or add a single note with Google Calendar or iPhone · Mac · Outlook in the note.

How do reminders work?

Open a note with a date and choose a reminder under the date. Reminders and the morning briefing arrive as notifications from the web app (sortmemo.com): the first time, allow notifications. On iPhone, open sortmemo.com in Safari and add it to your home screen first (Share → Add to Home Screen). Under your initials, 🔔 Notifications also has a morning briefing of your day. The free plan has 3 reminders a month.

The SortMemo apps from the App Store and Google Play let you choose a reminder on a note, but they don't show notifications yet: the reminder arrives through the web app on your home screen, if you have added it.

Can I get my notes back?

Premium keeps a backup of your notes every day for 30 days: tap your initials, then ⭐ Premium, and Restore. Everyone can download all their notes with ⬇ Download all my notes, under your initials in the web app at sortmemo.com.

How do I delete my account?

Tap your initials, then Delete my account at the bottom. Everything is deleted at once. More: Delete your account.
