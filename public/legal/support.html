Help and support

Write to [support@your-domain] or, in the app, tap your initials, then 💬 Send feedback. We answer within two working days.

Common questions

How do I make a checklist?

Start each line with - , or paste several lines and choose One note with a checklist.

How do I share a list with my partner?

Open the list and tap 👥 Share, or tap 👥 Share next to a group in the Groups view. Type their email and choose Can edit or View only.

The AI chose the wrong group or date.

Open the note and pick your own group, priority, date or time. Your choice always wins; the AI never changes it back.

How do I get my notes into my calendar?

Open a note with a date and tap Google Calendar or iPhone · Mac · Outlook. To see all dated notes in your calendar, tap your initials, then 📅 Calendar → Connect a calendar.

Can I get my notes back?

Premium keeps a backup of your notes every day for 30 days: tap your initials, then ⭐ Premium, and Restore. Everyone can download all their notes with ⬇ Download all my notes.

How do I delete my account?

Tap your initials, then Delete my account at the bottom. Everything is deleted at once.
