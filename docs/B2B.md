## record.ai for working parents: a benefit people use every day

**The problem.** Working parents carry two to-do lists in their head: the
job's and the family's. The mental load costs focus at work, and it is a top
reason parents, mostly mothers, cut hours or leave.

**What record.ai does.** One app for work and family. People write, dictate or
photograph anything: the AI sorts it into priorities, groups and dates, and
puts it next to their meetings (Google, Outlook, iCloud, Calendly). Lists are
shared with a partner in real time. A morning briefing shows the day.
Work and Family are kept apart with one switch, so private notes stay private.

**What the company gets.**
- A benefit that is used daily, not once a year
- Fewer missed things, less stress, for the people the company most wants to keep
- Private by design: the company never sees anyone's notes; only the number of people who use it
- Made and hosted in Europe (Frankfurt), GDPR-ready, in seven languages

**Price.** €3 per employee per month, billed yearly, for the employees who
activate it (each gets Family Premium for their household). [Minimum 20 seats?]

**Pilot.** 3 months free for up to [50] employees. At the end: how many
activated, how many use it weekly, and a short survey. If 30% or more use it
weekly, the plan continues at the price above.

**How it starts.** HR sends one email with a sign-up link; people join with
their work or personal email. Setup takes a day. [Contact: Nina Kozinska, email]
