# App Store and Google Play: the texts to paste

The step by step guide to publishing the iPhone and Android apps, and every
answer the store forms ask (App Privacy, Data safety, age rating, review
notes), is in **[ReleaseAppStore.md](ReleaseAppStore.md)**. This page holds
only the store texts, in the seven languages of the app.

Each language has a subtitle (App Store, 30 characters), keywords (App Store,
100 characters), a short description (Google Play, 80 characters) and a full
description (both stores). English and Italian also have the app's name in the
store (30 characters) and the "What's new" text for version 1.0.0. Native
speakers should read their language once before launch.

The pictures for Google Play (512×512 icon and 1024×500 feature graphic) are
in [docs/store/](store/); `node scripts/make-store-icons.mjs` makes them again
from `public/icons/icon.svg`.

Version 1.0.0 of the apps sells nothing inside the app, so the texts don't
mention prices or how to buy Premium (Apple and Google don't allow pointing
to another way to pay). They also leave out reminders and the morning
briefing: those arrive as notifications only from the web app for now.

## Store texts

### English

- **Name in the store:** SortMemo: AI Notes & Lists
- **Subtitle:** Notes and lists the AI sorts
- **Keywords:** notes,to do,family,lists,checklist,planner,shared,school,kids,groceries,labels,organizer,work,AI
- **Short description:** Write it down, the AI sorts it: notes, lists, dates and School for the family.
- **Full description:**

  Work and family, in one place. Write a note, dictate it or snap a photo of the school letter: SortMemo gives it a clear title, a group, a priority and its date, so the urgent things are always on top.

  • Lists you can tick: start lines with "- " or paste a list
  • Work, Family and School worlds: one switch to see only what matters now
  • School with one group per child; SortMemo asks "which child?" when a letter doesn't say
  • Turn a school letter or a photo into to-dos, each with its date
  • Labels like in Gmail, several per note, and search by label
  • Share a list, a group or all of School with your partner, live on every device
  • Hear your day read aloud, and suggested to-dos from your notes
  • Your calendars both ways: Google, Outlook, iCloud and Calendly
  • Works offline; syncs when you're back online
  • In English, Italian, French, Spanish, Portuguese, German and Polish

  Your notes stay yours: no ads, no selling data, and you can delete your account and everything in it from the app at any time. Hosted in Frankfurt, Germany.

- **What's new (1.0.0):** The first version of SortMemo for iPhone and Android: write, dictate or photograph notes, let the AI sort them, and keep work, family and school in order on every device.

## Before you paste: three things to check

- The calendar line only becomes true once the calendar keys are on the server (docs/CALENDARS.md). Until then, leave that line out.
- Reminders and the morning briefing are left out on purpose: on the iPhone and Android apps they need Apple's and Google's push keys first (docs/PARITY.md). Add the line back when the apps send notifications.
- The apps are free in the stores. If Premium is ever sold inside the apps, prices come from App Store Connect and Play Console, not from these texts.
