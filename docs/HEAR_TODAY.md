# Hear today

For the morning rush: the day's plan read aloud, in your language, without
opening a note. Every device reads the same text, made by the server
(`server/today.js`, `GET /api/today/spoken`):

> Good morning. Friday 6 November. At 7:30, School bag: Lunch box, Swim kit.
> At 9:00, Dentist for Leo. Also today: Buy milk. For you: Sign the trip form.
> Top to-do: Book the plumber. 1 overdue. Have a good day.

What it says, in order: things with a time, the rest of today, tasks someone
gave you, today's top suggested to-do, how many are overdue. A list's open
items are read after its title. At most eight things are read; the rest are
counted ("and 3 more"). The words, dates and times follow the language chosen
in the app (seven languages). Reading aloud is part of Premium.

## Where

| Device | How |
|---|---|
| Web (phone or computer) | The morning notification opens the app with a big **🔊 Hear today** button: one tap. The 🔊 on the Today line does the same. |
| iPhone | Say **"Hey Siri, hear today in SortNotes"** (also "Read my day…", "What's my day…"). Siri reads it without opening the app. The same action is in the Shortcuts app (**Hear today**), so it can go on the Action button or the Lock Screen. In the app: the 🔊 at the top. |
| Android | Long-press the SortNotes icon → **Hear today** (drag it to the home screen for one tap). In the app: the 🔊 at the top. |

The Siri phrases are in English for now; in another Siri language, use the
Shortcuts action (it is listed in every language).
